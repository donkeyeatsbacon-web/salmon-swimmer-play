Salmon Swimmer - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Salmon Swimmer is a game about a salmon swimming upstream. This policy covers the Android and iOS apps and the web version. In short: the game collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

What the game stores

Your progress (banked roe, unlocks, settings, your current run and your Journal of records and achievements) is saved only on your own device. In the web version it is kept in your browser's storage for this site. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or personal information of any kind.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection is used by the apps. (The web version is downloaded from GitHub Pages, whose own policy covers that download.)

Permissions

On Android the app asks only for vibration, used for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/donkeyeatsbacon-web/salmon-swimmer-play/issues.
